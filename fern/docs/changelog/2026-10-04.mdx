---
title: Flow builder refresh and voicemail detection pricing
---

{/* versions: api=0.1.288 app=0.1.65 voice=0.0.111 */}

- **Builder**: Refreshed the flow builder's color palette and node groups — nodes are grouped by what they do (**Voice**, **Transcriber**, **Model**) with real brand marks for ElevenLabs, Deepgram and Google Gemini. See [Choose models and voices](/build/choose-models-and-voices).
- **Calls**: Fixed a bug where picking up a transferred call in the dashboard could occasionally fail to connect you to the call.
- **Billing**: Voicemail detection moves from free to a small per-check charge — a typical check costs a fraction of a cent. See [Credits and pricing](/concepts/credits-and-pricing) and [Cost breakdown](/account/cost-breakdown).
