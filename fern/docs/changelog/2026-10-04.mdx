---
title: Flow builder refresh and voicemail detection pricing
---

{/* versions: api=0.1.288 app=0.1.65 voice=0.0.111 */}

- **Builder**: the flow builder has a new colour palette, and nodes are grouped by what they do — **Voice**, **Transcriber** and **Model** — with the ElevenLabs, Deepgram and Google Gemini marks. See [Choose models and voices](/build/choose-models-and-voices).
- **Calls**: fixed a bug where picking up a transferred call in the dashboard sometimes didn't connect you to the call.
- **Billing**: voicemail detection on outbound calls was free and is now charged by the tokens it uses — about \$0.00001 per call. It is its own line in the cost breakdown. See [Cost breakdown](/account/cost-breakdown).
