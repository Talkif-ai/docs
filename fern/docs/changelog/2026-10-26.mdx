---
title: Invitation resend, recording fixes, and microphone checks before test calls
---

{/* versions: api=0.1.343 app=0.1.103 voice=0.0.120 */}

- **Account**: resend a pending invitation from the **Team** page. The team card on the **Overview** and the Team page update live, and show when an away or offline status ends (for example "Away · 14:30") and who set it. See [Members and roles](/account/members-and-roles).
- **Calls**: fixed a bug where inbound calls to numbers from a connected SIP provider, and queued outbound calls through one, weren't recorded even with recording on.
- **Builder**: the test call asks for microphone access before it books an agent. A blocked, missing or busy microphone shows how to fix it, and a test call that fails during setup or that you hang up early is cancelled at once. See [Test and publish](/build/test-and-publish).
- **Telephony**: SIP provider addresses must be public. IP addresses, ranges and SIP domains that point to private, loopback or other internal addresses are refused when you add or change a provider. See [Bring your own SIP trunk](/telephony/bring-your-own-sip-trunk).
- **Telephony**: provider names saved as a placeholder such as "undefined" were cleared and are refused from now on; the dashboard shows the provider type instead.
- **API**: [`DELETE /calls/webrtc/{callId}`](/api-reference/calls/cancel-webrtc-call) cancels a browser call before its offer is sent. The agent reserved for the call is released at once, and an agent that never connected isn't billed.
- **API**: the available-countries and number-pricing responses no longer include `uri` and `url`. Both pointed at the carrier's own API and contained a carrier account identifier.
- **SDKs**: `@talkif/webrtc` 0.1.7 asks for the microphone before it creates the call. A denied, missing or busy microphone fails with `media-unavailable` before anything is created, instead of leaving an agent waiting for about a minute. See [Web calls](/sdks/browser).
