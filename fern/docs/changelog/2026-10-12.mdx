---
title: Custom alert rules and a notification inbox
---

{/* versions: api=0.1.323 app=0.1.88 voice=0.0.115 */}

- **Account**: Create custom alert rules that notify you by email, push or webhook when calls start failing, a function errors, or another condition you choose — every account now also gets a default "calls are failing" rule out of the box. Manage rules, incidents and everything else Talkif tells you from one Notifications page. See [Notifications](/account/notifications).
- **Account**: Alerts can also reach people outside your account — add an external email recipient and they get a confirmation link before anything is sent to them.
- **Account**: Removing a passkey now also removes it from your device's password manager, so it no longer shows up as a sign-in option that doesn't work. See [Passkeys](/account/passkeys-and-sign-in-with-apple#passkeys).
- **Account**: Accounts in their deletion grace period are now read-only — you can still cancel the deletion or export your data, but other changes are blocked until it's cancelled or the deletion completes. See [Data export and deletion](/account/data-export-and-deletion).
- **Account**: If Apple doesn't share your real email address on sign-up, you're now asked to provide one directly instead of using a private relay address. See [Sign in with Apple](/account/passkeys-and-sign-in-with-apple#sign-in-with-apple).
- **Calls**: Cancel a ringing call or end a live one directly from the calls table. See [Take calls in the dashboard](/calls/take-calls-in-the-dashboard).
- **Calls**: Call transfers and live takeover now work on WhatsApp voice calls. See [WhatsApp Business Calling](/telephony/whatsapp-business-calling).
- **Calls**: Fixed a bug where the agent leaving a call mid-transfer could leave the caller and the colleague disconnected from each other.
- **Billing**: A declined payment is no longer retried automatically — your balance reflects what's owed, and you can retry the charge from Billing when you're ready. See [Credits and payment](/account/credits-and-payment).
