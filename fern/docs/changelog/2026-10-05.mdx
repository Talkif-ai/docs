title: "Passkeys and Sign in with Apple"
---

{/* versions: api=0.1.292 app=0.1.72 voice=0.0.111 */}

- **Account**: sign in with a **passkey** — Touch ID, Face ID, Windows Hello or your device PIN, saved in iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager or your password manager. A passkey sign-in skips the 2FA code. Add one under **Security → Passkeys**; you get an email whenever a passkey is added or removed. See [Passkeys](/account/passkeys-and-sign-in-with-apple#passkeys).
- **Account**: **Continue with Apple** on sign-in and sign-up, with Hide My Email support. Existing users link Apple from **Security**; accounts are never merged by email. See [Sign in with Apple](/account/passkeys-and-sign-in-with-apple#sign-in-with-apple).
- **Security**: Google sign-in now asks for the two-factor code on users with two-factor authentication turned on.
