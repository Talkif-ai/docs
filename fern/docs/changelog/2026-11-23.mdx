title: Needs you page, transfer transcripts, call destinations
---

{/* versions: api=0.1.410 app=0.1.130 voice=0.0.141 */}

- **Calls**: turn on transcribing a transferred call for the time after your agent hands it to a person, per flow. See [Transfer calls to people](/build/transfer-calls-to-people).
- **Calls**: a transferred call's transcript now labels your teammate's lines with their name, and call analysis no longer mistakes a teammate's words for the caller's.
- **Telephony**: choose which countries your agent's outbound calls may reach, from account settings. A call to a country you haven't allowed, or whose country can't be determined, is refused before it dials. See [Inbound and outbound routing](/telephony/inbound-and-outbound-routing).
- **Dashboard**: new **Needs you** page lists calls with something still open after the call ended, so you can follow up and mark each one done or dismiss it.
- **Dashboard**: the contact list can be sorted and filtered by when a contact was last called. See [Contacts](/calls/contacts).
- **Assistant**: Ask Talkif can mark a **Needs you** item done or dismiss it, and can run more actions directly from chat — scheduling, campaigns, contacts, templates and Do Not Call — each confirmation naming the real record it will change. See [Assistant](/build/assistant).
- **Assistant**: Ask Talkif can list your invoices.
- **Billing**: **Billing → Invoices** lists every invoice with its own page to view or download as a PDF, including the invoice number printed on it. See [Credits and payment](/account/credits-and-payment).
- **Account**: report a reply from Ask Talkif or an agent's line in a call transcript that looks wrong, from the chat or the transcript itself.
