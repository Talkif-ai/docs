If a firewall sits between Talkif and something you run — a SIP carrier, a webhook endpoint, an internal API — this is the page to hand your network team. Three kinds of traffic, three different sets of addresses; conflating them is the usual cause of "signalling works but there's no audio" and "the webhook never arrives".

## Talkif's endpoints (you → Talkif)

| Endpoint | Purpose | Port |
|---|---|---|
| `api.talkif.ai` | REST API, real-time WebSocket (`/api/v1/ws/events`) | 443 |
| `app.talkif.ai` | dashboard | 443 |
| `sip.talkif.ai` → **`63.176.211.13`** | SIP signalling for your carrier's trunk, EU region | 5060 (UDP / TCP / TLS as configured) |
| `sip.us.talkif.ai` → **`44.218.212.38`** | SIP signalling for your carrier's trunk, US region | 5060 (UDP / TCP / TLS as configured) |

`api.talkif.ai` and `app.talkif.ai` are served from both regions behind standard HTTPS; there's nothing to allowlist outbound beyond port 443 to those names.

## SIP trunks (your carrier ↔ Talkif)

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Signalling" defaultOpen>
    Talkif has one SIP address per region: `63.176.211.13` (EU, `sip.talkif.ai`) and `44.218.212.38` (US, `sip.us.talkif.ai`), port 5060. Outbound INVITEs come **from** the address of the SIP provider's region, chosen from where the carrier's IPs are; inbound INVITEs can be sent **to** either address, and the call is handled in that region. Have your carrier allow both addresses if you're not sure which region your provider is in. Talkif accepts inbound SIP only from the carrier IPs/CIDRs listed on your SIP provider in the dashboard, and those must be public addresses. Talkif does not REGISTER with your carrier.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Media (RTP)">
    Media doesn't flow through the signalling address. On a SIP trunk, audio is exchanged with Talkif's media servers in the region that handles the call, on UDP ports **40000–49999**; each call's SDP gives the address to send it to. These addresses aren't fixed, and a region can use more than one, so don't limit RTP to a fixed list of Talkif addresses — let your carrier send media to the address in the SDP. Symptom of getting this wrong: calls connect, nobody hears anything (or only one side does).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Full setup in [Bring your own SIP trunk](/telephony/bring-your-own-sip-trunk); how the edge is chosen in [Regions and edges](/telephony/regions-and-edges).

## Webhooks and flow functions (Talkif → you)

HTTPS requests from Talkif to your endpoints — flow functions called during a call — leave through a **single fixed egress address per region** (EU and US). They are the addresses to allowlist if your endpoint isn't open to the internet.

The current addresses are provided by support on request and confirmed in writing, so that a change is communicated rather than discovered. Two things to design in regardless of source IP:

- **Verify the `Talkif-Signature` header** on every request — [Webhooks](/integrate/webhooks). An allowlist proves where a request came from; the signature proves who sent it and that it's intact.
- **Allow both regions.** A call served from the US region calls your endpoint from the US egress address even if your account "lives" in the EU.

Requests are made only to **public** addresses: private ranges (`10/8`, `172.16/12`, `192.168/16`), loopback, link-local and cloud-metadata addresses are refused, and hostnames resolving to any such address are refused too. Redirects are not followed.
