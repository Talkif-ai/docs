Talkif is an AI voice agent platform. Build conversation flows visually, connect them to phone numbers, and run intelligent voice calls at scale.

## What you can do

- **Build conversation flows** — Drag-and-drop flow builder with AI agent nodes, provider configuration, and action triggers
- **Make and receive calls** — Purchase phone numbers or bring your own carrier, assign flows, and go live
- **Run campaigns** — Bulk outbound calling with scheduling, call windows, and real-time monitoring
- **Track everything** — Full transcripts, recordings, per-call cost breakdowns, and live dashboards
