---
title: Credits
description: How Talkif credits work, including purchasing, expiration, and real-time deduction.
---

Credits are the currency of Talkif. You purchase credits in USD and spend them on calls.

## Purchasing credits

Add credits to your account at any time from the Billing page. Credits are purchased in dollar amounts and added to your balance instantly.

## Credit types

| Type | Expires | Consumed |
|---|---|---|
| **Subscription** | End of billing period | First |
| **Promotional** | Optional expiration date | Second |
| **Bonus** | Never | Third |
| **Purchased** | Never | Last |

Credits with earlier expiration are consumed first to minimize waste.

Promotional and bonus credits can fund calls, but not phone number purchases or rentals — those require purchased or subscription balance.

## Expiration

- **Purchased credits** never expire. Your balance carries forward indefinitely.
- **Promotional credits** may have expiration dates. Expiration is displayed on the credit when applicable.
- **Bonus credits** from referrals or rewards never expire.

## Real-time deduction

Balance is reserved in real time while a call runs, and the final charge settles at call end. Your balance updates live during active calls so you always see your current position.

## Balance display

Your current credit balance is visible in the dashboard header at all times. If your balance reaches zero during a call, the call will end.
