Templates are pre-built flows that give you a working starting point. Select a template, customize the prompts and settings, and publish.

## Available templates

### Appointment Reminder

Automated appointment reminder calls with confirmation options.

**Includes:**
- Main Agent configured as a friendly appointment reminder assistant — reminds the customer about their upcoming appointment and confirms they can attend
- OpenAI LLM provider (`gpt-4.1-mini`)
- ElevenLabs TTS provider (`eleven_turbo_v2`) — you pick the voice when creating the flow
- Deepgram STT provider (`nova-2-conversationalai`)

## Using a template

1. Open the Flow Builder and click **New Flow**
2. Select a template from the template gallery
3. The template is cloned into a new draft — all nodes, edges, and configuration are copied
4. Customize prompts, provider settings, and transitions for your use case
5. Test and publish when ready

Templates are starting points. After cloning, every node and connection is fully editable — add nodes, remove nodes, change prompts, swap providers. The template structure is a suggestion, not a constraint.

## Saving your own templates

Any flow can be saved as an account template. Account templates appear in your template gallery alongside system templates and are only visible to your account.
