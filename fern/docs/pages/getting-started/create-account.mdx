## Sign up

1. Go to [app.talkif.ai](https://app.talkif.ai) and click **Sign Up**
2. Enter your **name**, **email address**, and **phone number**
3. Create a **password**
4. Review and accept the **[Terms of Service](https://trust.talkif.ai/terms)** and **[Privacy Policy](https://trust.talkif.ai/privacy)**
5. Click **Create Account**

<Info>
  Your account starts with a **$5.00 welcome credit** to explore the platform.
</Info>

### Google OAuth

Alternatively, click **Continue with Google** to sign up with one click using your Google account. This skips the email verification step.

## Verify your identity

### Email verification

After signing up, check your inbox for a verification email from Talkif. Click the verification link to confirm your email address.

<Info>
  If you don't see the email, check your spam folder. The link expires after 24 hours.
</Info>

### Phone verification

You'll receive an SMS with a 6-digit verification code. Enter the code on the verification screen to confirm your phone number.

## Log in

Once verified, log in at [app.talkif.ai](https://app.talkif.ai) with your email and password, or use **Continue with Google**.

## Multiple organizations

A single Talkif account can belong to multiple organizations. After logging in, you can switch between organizations from the sidebar. If you've been invited to an existing organization, the invitation will appear after your first login.
