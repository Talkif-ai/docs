Welcome to Talkif. This guide walks you through account setup to making your first live voice call.

## What you'll learn

- **Create your account** — sign up, verify your identity, and log in
- **Understand the platform** — how Flows, Agents, Providers, and Phone Numbers work together
- **Make your first call** — build a flow, assign it to a number, and test it live

## Prerequisites

- A web browser
- An email address
- A phone number (for verification and testing)
