Talkif is an AI voice agent platform. You build visual conversation flows, assign them to phone numbers, and run AI-powered voice calls — no code required.

## Core concepts

### Flows

A Flow is the visual conversation logic for your AI agent. You build Flows in a drag-and-drop builder by connecting nodes that define how the AI thinks, speaks, and listens.

### Agents

An Agent is the AI personality within a Flow. It has a **system prompt** (who it is) and a **task prompt** (what it should do). The Main Agent is the entry point for every call.

### Providers

Providers are the AI services that power your agent:

- **LLM** — the language model that generates responses (e.g., GPT-4o, Claude)
- **TTS** — text-to-speech that gives your agent a voice (e.g., ElevenLabs)
- **STT** — speech-to-text that transcribes caller speech (e.g., Deepgram)

### Phone Numbers

Phone Numbers are the entry points for calls. You purchase numbers directly in Talkif and assign published Flows to them.

## How a call works

1. An inbound call hits a **Phone Number**
2. The number triggers its assigned **Flow**
3. The **Main Agent** processes the conversation using its prompts
4. **Providers** handle AI reasoning (LLM), speech synthesis (TTS), and transcription (STT)
5. The call ends and a full transcript, recording, and cost breakdown are available in the **Call Center**

## Billing

Talkif uses **credits-based billing**. You pay as you go — no subscriptions. Each call's cost is broken down by provider usage (LLM tokens, TTS characters, STT duration) so you know exactly where your credits go.

## Real-time monitoring

The Call Center shows live call status, real-time transcripts, and cost tracking for every active and completed call.

## Organizations

Talkif supports team collaboration through organizations. Invite team members, assign roles, and share Flows, numbers, and billing across your team.
