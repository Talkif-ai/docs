Organizations are the top-level entity for team collaboration on Talkif. Create an organization to share flows, phone numbers, contacts, and billing across your team.

## Key concepts

- **Shared resources** — all flows, phone numbers, contacts, and credits belong to the organization
- **Role-based access** — Owner, Admin, and Member roles control who can do what
- **Invite-based membership** — add team members by email with a specific role
