title: Invitations
description: Invite team members by email and manage pending invitations.
---

## Inviting members

<Info>
  Only **Owners** and **Admins** can invite new members.
</Info>

1. Navigate to **Settings → Organizations → Members**
2. Click **Invite Member**
3. Enter the invitee's email address
4. Select a role — **Admin** or **Member**
5. Send the invitation

## What the invitee sees

- **Existing Talkif user** — the invitation appears in their dashboard. They click accept to join.
- **New to Talkif** — they receive an email with a sign-up link. After creating their account, they are automatically added to the organization.

## Managing invitations

- **Pending invitations** are listed under the Members tab
- **Revoke** a pending invitation at any time before it's accepted
- Invitees can **decline** an invitation

## Leaving an organization

Members can leave an organization voluntarily from **Settings → Organizations**. The Owner cannot leave without first transferring ownership to another member.
