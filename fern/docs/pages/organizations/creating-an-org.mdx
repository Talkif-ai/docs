## Create an organization

1. Navigate to **Settings → Organizations**
2. Click **Create Organization**
3. Enter your organization name
4. Confirm

You are automatically assigned the **Owner** role. The organization is active immediately.

## What belongs to the organization

All resources are scoped to the organization:

- **Flows** — conversation flows built in the Flow Builder
- **Phone numbers** — purchased or connected numbers
- **Contacts** — your contact lists
- **Credits** — billing and credit balance

When you create resources, they belong to the organization, not your personal account.
