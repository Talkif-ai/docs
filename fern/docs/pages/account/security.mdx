Your user is the key to every account you belong to — including the organization's balance and its phone lines. Securing it is not optional for anyone with the Owner role, and it's cheap: two-factor authentication takes a minute to set up and is required on every sign-in afterwards.

Everything on this page is **per user**, not per account: it follows you across every account you can access. Find it in the user menu (top right): **Profile**, **Preferences**, **Security** and **Sessions**.

## Profile

Your name, email address and phone number, and which are verified. You can change your name here; your email address can't be changed.

Verification goes in a fixed order — **email first, then phone**:

1. **Email**: a 6-digit code sent to your address, valid for 10 minutes. If you signed up with Google or Apple, your email is already verified.
2. **Phone**: a 6-digit code by SMS, valid for 10 minutes. Talkif sends it only after your email is verified.

What verification unlocks, for the **account Owner**:

| The Owner has verified | Then the account can |
|---|---|
| Email **or** phone | Place calls (browser calls included) and start campaigns and schedules |
| Email **and** phone | Also buy phone numbers, create API keys and invite members |

To change your phone number, click the edit button next to it on **Profile**. If a number is already set, Talkif asks for your current password first (set a password if you don't have one); then it sends a code to the new number.

## Signing in

Several ways, and you can have any combination on one user. Google and Apple are linked or unlinked under **Security → Connected Accounts**.

- **Email and password.** Change it under **Security → Password**; the current password is required, and the new one needs at least 8 characters. Changing or resetting your password **signs you out on every other device** and in any app connected to your Talkif login. A user created through Google or Apple can set a password here to sign in either way.
- **Continue with Google.** Links your Google identity to the user. Google handles the credential; if two-factor authentication is on, Talkif still asks for the code after Google.
- **Continue with Apple.** Signs in with your Apple ID. Two-factor authentication applies the same way. See [Sign in with Apple](/account/passkeys-and-sign-in-with-apple#sign-in-with-apple).
- **Passkey.** Unlocks with Touch ID, Face ID, Windows Hello or the device PIN, and skips the 2FA code because it already proves both the device and you. See [Passkeys](/account/passkeys-and-sign-in-with-apple#passkeys).

If our Terms or Privacy Policy changed since you last accepted them, you're asked to accept the new version right after signing in, whichever way you signed in.

### Limits that protect your login

- **Wrong passwords.** After several wrong passwords in a row, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes. Wait, then try again — or reset the password.
- **Confirming it's you.** Adding a passkey, turning off two-factor authentication and changing your password or phone ask you to confirm first. After several wrong attempts these pause for 15 minutes: *"Too many attempts. Try again later."*
- **Codes and links by email.** Sign-up codes, password resets and other emailed codes are limited per address per hour. If you hit the limit, wait an hour before asking again.

### The security check

Sign-up, sign-in, password reset and the pages that send you a verification code run a short Cloudflare security check against automated abuse. Usually you don't notice it; sometimes it asks you to tick a box. If it can't load, the page says *"The security check could not run"*: allow `challenges.cloudflare.com` in your content blocker, or refresh the page, and try again.

## Two-factor authentication

Time-based one-time passwords (TOTP), compatible with Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password and any standard app. Your user needs a password first — without one, the setting says *"Set a password first to enable 2FA"*.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable">
    **User menu → Security → Two-Factor Authentication → Authenticator App → Enable.** Confirm with your password, scan the QR code with your app (or enter the code shown under *Can't scan?*), and enter the six-digit code it shows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the backup codes">
    You get **10 single-use backup codes** (8 characters each, shown as `XXXX-XXXX`). They're the only way back in if you lose the phone. Put them in a password manager now — they are not shown again. **Backup Codes** on the same page makes a new set (with your password); the old codes stop working.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with it">
    From then on, every password, Google or Apple sign-in asks for the current code, or a backup code. A passkey sign-in doesn't: the passkey is already a second factor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To disable, click **Disable** and confirm with your current password. If you're an organization Owner, consider it mandatory; you're the only person who can change the organization's billing.

## Sessions

Two buttons end your sign-ins:

- **User menu → Sessions → Sign Out All Others**
- **User menu → Security → Sign Out Everywhere → Sign Out All**

Both end every sign-in of your user, **including the one in this browser**, and disconnect apps connected to your Talkif login; sign in again afterwards. Other devices are signed out once their current session expires, shortly after. The Sessions page shows this device; other sessions aren't listed. Use this after a lost laptop or a suspected compromise — and change your password too.

## Deleting your login

**User menu → Security → Delete my login** removes you as a Talkif user, with a seven-day grace period in which you can cancel. Your own workspaces, and organizations where you're the only member, are deleted with you. See [Deleting your login](/account/data-export-and-deletion#deleting-your-login).

## Ad measurement

Talkif advertises on platforms such as ChatGPT. **Ad measurement** decides whether the dashboard tells the ad platform that brought you to Talkif about your sign-up and later steps such as calls, numbers and payments, with a hashed email address. The [Privacy Policy](https://trust.talkif.ai/privacy#144-advertising-measurement) lists exactly what is sent and to whom.

It's a **personal setting**, not an account setting: it follows you into every account you open. Find it under **User menu → Preferences → Privacy → Ad measurement**.

| Where you are | Until November 14, 2026 | From November 15, 2026 (UTC) |
|---|---|---|
| EU/EEA, the UK, Switzerland, Turkey | off unless you turn it on | off unless you turn it on |
| Everywhere else | off unless you turn it on | **on by default**; turn it off any time |

- In the regions where you must opt in, sign-up shows an unticked, optional checkbox. Ticking it is the only way sign-up turns it on.
- A choice you've made — on or off — is kept and always wins over the default.
- If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, ad measurement stays off.
- Under California law this may count as "sharing" personal information; you can opt out at any time with the same setting.

The dashboard sets no analytics or advertising cookies of its own and shows no cookie banner. While ad measurement is on, the ad platform's tag can set its own cookies.
