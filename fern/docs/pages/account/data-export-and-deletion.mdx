description: Take everything out of Talkif as one JSON file, and close an account with an emailed confirmation and a seven-day grace period during which the account is read-only.

Two things you should be able to do with any service that holds your customers' calls: leave with your data, and leave for good. Both are self-service, both are done by the account **Owner**, and both are gated by an emailed confirmation so a stolen session can't trigger them. Deletion adds a week in which you can change your mind.

## Exporting your data

One request produces a single JSON file with every category of data the account holds — contacts, calls with transcripts, flows, numbers, campaigns, schedules, billing history — minus secrets (passwords, MFA secrets, carrier credentials). It's built for portability: structured, machine-readable, and the same shapes the API uses, so it loads into another system or your own warehouse without translation.

## What's included

The export covers 16 data categories:

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Account** | Account metadata (name, slug, type, status) |
| **Users** | User profiles (passwords, MFA secrets and backup codes excluded) |
| **Contacts** | Full contact records |
| **Calls** | Call records with PII intact (calls already scrubbed by your retention settings excluded) |
| **Flows** | AI conversation flows |
| **Phone numbers** | Numbers owned by your account |
| **VoIP providers** | Your carrier connections (credentials and settings excluded) |
| **Call schedules** | Recurring schedule definitions |
| **Campaigns** | Bulk campaign records |
| **Charges** | Billing history |
| **Cost line items** | Detailed cost breakdown per charge |
| **Balance transactions** | Balance change history |
| **Notifications** | Notification history |
| **Connectors message log** | Messages exchanged through Connectors |
| **Connectors call memory** | What Connectors kept from your calls |
| **Connectors terms acceptance** | When the account accepted the Connectors terms |

The output is a single JSON file structured by category:

```json
{
  "exportVersion": "1.0",
  "accountId": "<your-account-id>",
  "exportedAt": "2026-02-28T12:00:00Z",
  "data": {
    "account": [...],
    "users": [...],
    "contacts": [...],
    "calls": [...]
  }
}
```

## How to request an export

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request it">
    **Settings → General → Data Export → Request export.** Only the Owner sees this section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm by email">
    Click the link in the confirmation email. It expires after **24 hours**; if it does, request a new export.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for it">
    The section shows **Export in progress** and how many of the 16 categories are done. It usually takes a few minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download it">
    You get an email when it's ready. Open **Settings → General → Data Export** and click **Download**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Download window

The file is available for **24 hours** after it's ready — the section shows *Download expires* with the time. Each click on **Download** creates a fresh link, so you can download it more than once in that window. After that, the file is permanently deleted.

## Limits

- **One active export at a time** — you can't request a new export while one is awaiting confirmation, in progress, or ready to download.
- **One request per 24 hours** — counted from the previous request. A request that failed or expired doesn't count.

## What the Data Export section shows

| It says | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Awaiting confirmation** · *Check email* | Requested; click the link in the email to start |
| **Export in progress** | Collecting and packaging the data |
| **Export ready** · **Download** | Ready; downloadable until *Download expires* |
| **Retry export**, with the error | The export failed — request it again |
| **Request export** | No export, or the last one expired |

## Security

- Exports require email confirmation before processing starts
- Sensitive fields (passwords, MFA secrets, carrier credentials) are automatically excluded
- Download links are short-lived and unique to your account
- Export files are encrypted at rest and deleted after the download window expires

## GDPR data portability

This feature fulfills the right to data portability under [GDPR Article 20](https://trust.talkif.ai/privacy). Your data is provided in a structured, machine-readable JSON format suitable for transfer to another service.

## Deleting the account

Account deletion is permanent. To prevent accidental data loss, deletion uses a two-step confirmation with a grace period. Only the Owner can request or cancel it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request it">
    **Settings → General → Delete Account → Delete account → Send confirmation email.** The link in the email expires after **24 hours**. Until you click it, the section says **Awaiting confirmation**, with a **Cancel** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it">
    Clicking the link schedules the deletion for **7 days** later and emails you the date. From now on the account is **read-only** — see below. Export your data now if you haven't.
  </Step>
  <Step title="After seven days">
    Deletion runs and can't be undone. You get a final email when it's done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### While a deletion is pending, the account is read-only

From the moment you confirm until the deletion runs or is cancelled, every page shows a banner: *"This account is scheduled for deletion on … Changes are paused until the deletion is canceled."*

| Still works | Paused |
|---|---|
| Signing in and looking at everything | Every change: flows, contacts, settings, numbers, members, API keys |
| Exporting your data | Calls of any kind, inbound included |
| Cancelling the deletion | Campaigns and schedules |
| | Top-ups and auto-recharge |

A blocked change shows the same message. API requests that would change something are refused with `409 account_pending_deletion`.

### Cancelling a pending deletion

You can cancel at any stage before the deletion runs:

- **Before you confirm** — click **Cancel** in **Settings → General**, or let the link expire after 24 hours.
- **During the grace period** — click **Cancel deletion** in the banner or in **Settings → General**.

Cancellation restores the account to normal at once. Members who aren't the Owner see *"Ask an owner to cancel it."*

### What deletion does

- Active calls are hung up; campaigns and schedules are cancelled; carrier connections are switched off.
- **Talkif numbers** are released and can't be recovered.
- **Numbers in your own Twilio account stay in your Twilio account.** Talkif only removes its own call settings from them.
- **Numbers on your own SIP trunk** stop being routed to Talkif.
- Account data is removed from Talkif at once and permanently erased within **90 days**.
- **Billing and tax records** — charges, cost line items, balance history, payments and refunds — are kept for **7 years**, as the law requires, and charges for usage before deletion still stand.

<Warning title="Deleting a personal account affects your login">
  - **The personal account created when you signed up:** deleting it ends your login. When the deletion runs, you're signed out everywhere and your passkeys and Google and Apple sign-in are removed. When its data is purged, 60 days later, your email address, password, phone number and two-factor settings are erased too, and you can no longer sign in to any account — including organizations you belong to.
  - **Any other personal account you own:** when the deletion runs, you're signed out everywhere and your passkeys and Google and Apple sign-in are removed. Your password keeps working.
</Warning>

### If the deletion can't run yet

A deletion waits while the account has an **unpaid or disputed charge**. Nothing is deleted, the account stays read-only, and Talkif tries again every day. Settle the charge — [contact support](mailto:support@talkif.ai) if you need help — or cancel the deletion.

### Before you delete

1. **Export your data** — the export above, before you confirm.
2. **Check billing** — outstanding charges survive deletion, and an unpaid one holds it up.
3. **Notify your team** — if this is an organization's account, every member loses access when it runs.

<Warning>
  After the 7-day grace period, account deletion cannot be reversed. Make sure you've downloaded any data you need.
</Warning>

## After the account is closed

- **Final export.** Contact legal@talkif.ai within 30 days of closure to request one.
- **Remaining balance.** Prepaid balance may be refunded on closure, less fees and outstanding charges, if you ask within 30 days. See the [Terms](https://trust.talkif.ai/terms), section 5.8.

For an organization, the Owner deletes the organization's account the same way, from that account's **Settings → General**. Members lose access when it runs.
