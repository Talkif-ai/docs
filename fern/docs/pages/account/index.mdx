Two kinds of thing live here. **Billing** is about the account's money: topping up, cards, auto-recharge, and understanding where every cent went. **Settings** is about the people and the protection: who's a member and what they can do, how you sign in, what you're told about, and how to leave with your data.

A quick orientation to the model, because it affects where things are:

- A **user** is a person who signs in. A user can belong to several accounts and switches between them.
- An **account** owns everything operational: flows, numbers, contacts, calls, and the credit balance. API keys are per account.
- An **organization** is a team that owns an account and has **members** with roles. A personal account has no organization; create one when a second person needs access.
