---
title: Members and roles
description: The three roles in an organization — Owner, Admin, Member — exactly what each may do, how invitations work, and how to suspend, remove or hand over.
---

Roles answer one question: *who can spend the account's money, and who can change who's in the room?* Everything operational — building flows, placing calls, managing contacts and campaigns — is open to every member, because that's the work. What's gated is billing, telephony purchases, membership, and the account itself.

## The roles

<ParamField path="Owner" type="exactly one">
  Created the organization or was handed it. The only role that touches money and the account's existence: billing and cards, buying and releasing numbers, connecting carriers, account settings, deletion, changing members' roles, transferring ownership.
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="Admin" type="any number">
  Runs the team without touching the money: invites, suspends and removes members (not the Owner), plus everything a Member can do.
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="Member" type="any number">
  Does the work: flows, prompts, calls, campaigns, schedules, contacts, DNC, connecting flows to numbers, reading transcripts and costs.
</ParamField>

## What each role can do

| Action | Owner | Admin | Member |
|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Build, test, publish flows; manage prompts and flow functions | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Place calls; run campaigns and schedules; read transcripts, recordings, insights | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Manage contacts, imports, the Do Not Call list | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Connect or disconnect a flow on a number; set a number's friendly name and recording override | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| View costs and per-call cost breakdowns | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Buy or release numbers; add, edit or remove carriers (SIP, Twilio, WhatsApp); register BYOC numbers** | ✓ | — | — |
| **Top up; manage cards; auto-recharge; invoices; balance and transactions** | ✓ | — | — |
| **Account settings (recording, analysis, notifications thresholds)** | ✓ | — | — |
| Invite members; suspend or remove a member | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| **Change a member's role** | ✓ | — | — |
| **Transfer ownership** | ✓ | — | — |
| **Delete the organization and its account** | ✓ | — | — |

API keys are created and revoked by the **Owner** and act with the scopes they're given regardless of who uses them.

## Inviting someone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Settings → General → Members → Invite Member">
    Enter their email, choose **Admin** or **Member** (no one is invited as Owner — ownership is transferred, not granted), and optionally a title and department for the member list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They accept">
    An email carries the invitation. If they already have a Talkif user, it also appears when they next sign in. Until accepted, the member shows as **Pending**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They switch to the account">
    Accepting adds the organization's account to their account switcher. Their personal account, if any, is untouched.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Suspending, removing, leaving

- **Suspend** keeps the member on the list but blocks access — the right move for a leaver whose work you still need to audit. **Reactivate** reverses it.
- **Remove** takes them off the organization. Their user account and personal account continue to exist; anything they created in the organization's account stays there.
- A member can **leave** on their own. The Owner can't leave without first transferring ownership.
- The Owner can't be removed by anyone; transfer ownership first.

## Transferring ownership

The Owner picks an existing active member; that member becomes Owner and the previous Owner becomes an Admin. Do this before an Owner leaves the company — an organization without a reachable Owner can't change its billing or delete itself without support's help.
