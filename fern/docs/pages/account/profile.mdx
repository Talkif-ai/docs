Navigate to **Settings → Account → Profile** to manage your personal information.

## Editable fields

- **Name** — displayed across the platform (in call logs, member lists, etc.)

## Phone number

Your phone number can be changed via a verified flow:

1. Navigate to **Settings → Account → Profile**
2. Click **Change** next to your phone number
3. Enter the new number (E.164 format)
4. Enter your current password to verify your identity
5. A verification code is sent to the new number
6. Enter the code to confirm the change

The previous number is released immediately upon confirmation.

## Read-only fields

- **Email** — set during signup, cannot be changed

## Google account linking

Link your Google account to enable one-click login with Google.

- **Link** — connect your Google account from the Profile settings page
- **Unlink** — disconnect a previously linked Google account

Linking Google does not change your email or password. It adds an additional login method.
