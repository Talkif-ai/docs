title: Account Deletion
description: Permanently delete your account with a confirmation step and 7-day grace period.
---

Navigate to **Settings → General** to manage account deletion.

## Delete your account

Account deletion is permanent. To prevent accidental data loss, deletion uses a two-step confirmation with a grace period.

### Step 1: Request deletion

1. Go to **Settings → General**
2. Click **Delete Account**
3. Check your email for a confirmation link

The confirmation link expires after **24 hours**. If it expires, you'll need to request deletion again.

### Step 2: Confirm deletion

Click the confirmation link in your email. Your account enters a **7-day grace period** — deletion is scheduled but not yet executed.

During the grace period:
- Your account remains accessible
- You can cancel the deletion at any time from Settings
- You should [export your data](/account/data-export) if you haven't already

### After the grace period

Once the 7-day grace period ends, the deletion is irreversible:
- All account data is permanently removed
- Active phone numbers are released
- Running campaigns and schedules are terminated
- Billing obligations for usage before deletion still apply

## Cancel a pending deletion

You can cancel at any stage before the final deletion executes:

- **Before email confirmation** — the request simply expires after 24 hours
- **During the grace period** — go to **Settings → General** and click **Cancel Deletion**

Cancellation restores your account to its normal active state immediately.

## Before you delete

1. **Export your data** — use [Data Export](/account/data-export) to download a copy of your account data
2. **Check billing** — outstanding balance obligations survive deletion
3. **Notify your team** — if you're in an organization, coordinate with other members

<Warning>
  After the 7-day grace period, account deletion cannot be reversed. Make sure you've downloaded any data you need.
</Warning>

## Post-termination export

If you need your data after account deletion, contact legal@talkif.ai within 30 days of termination to request a final export.
