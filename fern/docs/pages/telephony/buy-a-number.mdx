The simplest way to get a line is to buy one from Talkif. The number is provisioned on Talkif's own carrier, appears in your account within seconds, and needs no configuration beyond connecting a flow. It can answer inbound calls and place outbound ones, and it renews from your balance every 30 days until you release it.

## What you're buying

A **local**, **mobile** or **toll-free** number, depending on what the country sells — the dashboard offers only the types a country has. Every Talkif number is a voice line. The rental price depends on the country and number type; the search results show it per number before you commit.

<Markdown src="/docs/snippets/generated/pricing-phone-numbers.mdx" />

The purchase charge is the number's first 30 days of rental; the same amount then renews every 30 days.

Some countries require an address or identity documentation for regulatory reasons; a number's `addressRequirements` says whether it needs one (`none`, `any`, `local`, `foreign`). Where a requirement exists, the carrier needs that documentation on file before the purchase goes through — contact support before buying in that country.

Per-minute call charges are separate from rental. On Talkif numbers they're the carrier's rate for the number called, so they vary by country: see [Credits and pricing](/concepts/credits-and-pricing#telephony).

## Who can buy, and how many

- **The Owner buys and releases numbers**, signed in to the dashboard. API keys can search for numbers but can't buy them.
- **The Owner's email and phone number must be verified** first. Until they are, buying is refused with *"The account owner must verify their phone number"* (or email address). See [Security](/account/security#profile).
- **At most 5 purchases in any 24 hours** — a rolling window, and released numbers count toward it.

How many Talkif numbers an account can hold depends on whether it has paid:

| | Talkif numbers the account can hold |
|---|---|
| Before the account's first payment | 2 |
| After the first payment | 10 |

Every number not yet released counts, suspended ones included; your own Twilio, SIP and WhatsApp numbers don't. Need more? Contact support — the limit can be raised per account.

When a limit stops a purchase, the error says which one: `phone_number_limit_reached`, `phone_purchase_rate_limited` (with when to try again) or `owner_verification_required`.

## Paying for it

Two rules that surprise people:

- **Until the account's first payment, numbers are paid only from money you've added.** Bonus and promotional credit — including the welcome credit — can't buy or rent a number on an account that has never paid; the purchase is refused as *Not enough balance* (API: `insufficient_balance` — *"Trial and free credit can be used for phone numbers only after your first payment."*). After your first top-up, any credit pays for numbers, in the [usual order](/concepts/credits-and-pricing#kinds-of-balance).
- **Rental renews automatically every 30 days**, from the day of purchase (or from the day the number was reactivated), against the same balance. The number's `monthlyCost` and `billingStatus` are on the record.

### What happens when rental can't be paid

<Steps>
  <Step title="Past due">
    The renewal couldn't be charged. The number's billing status becomes **`past_due`**, it keeps working, and the charge is retried every hour. You have **7 days** from the renewal date to top up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Suspended">
    Still unpaid after 7 days, the number is **suspended**: it can't place calls. The Owner and admins are notified that unpaid numbers are released after 14 days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Release notice">
    At least **3 days** before the release, they're told the date: *"Unless you add funds, it will be released on … and can't be recovered after that."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Released">
    **14 days** after it was suspended, the number is released and can't be recovered — about three weeks after the missed renewal. A number that's the caller ID for a Meta lead form mapping is kept suspended instead, and you're told once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Getting it back.** Add money — a card top-up or an auto-recharge — at any point before the release. Each suspended number is reactivated automatically, oldest first, and charged a fresh 30 days of rental from that day; the suspended days aren't charged. Numbers the balance can't cover stay suspended. Free credit, or a balance that already covers the rent, doesn't reactivate a number by itself — it takes a payment.

<Accordion title="Billing statuses">
  <ParamField path="active" type="billingStatus">Rental is paid up.</ParamField>
  <ParamField path="processing" type="billingStatus">A renewal charge is in progress.</ParamField>
  <ParamField path="past_due" type="billingStatus">Renewal failed for insufficient balance; inside the 7-day grace period. Calls still work.</ParamField>
  <ParamField path="suspended" type="billingStatus">Grace period elapsed unpaid. Reactivated by a payment; released 14 days after suspension.</ParamField>
</Accordion>

## Buying

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Phone & WhatsApp → Add Number → Talkif System Provider">
        *Purchase new numbers (we handle payment)* opens **Purchase Number**. Pick a country from the list of those available for purchase, and a number type the country sells.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Search">
        **Contains Pattern** filters by digits anywhere: `*` matches one digit, `%` any sequence, wildcards only at the start or end (`555%` for a prefix, `%1234` for a suffix). For the US and Canada you can also filter by **Area Code**, and under **Advanced Filters** by **State**, **City** and **ZIP Code**. Tick **Exclude address-required numbers** to skip numbers that need regulatory paperwork, and choose 20, 30 or 50 results. Each search returns a fresh random batch from the carrier's inventory; search again to see more.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Purchase">
        Expand a number to see its recurring charge, then click **Purchase**. The first 30 days are charged immediately, the number is provisioned and named after its city and state, and it appears in your list as **Active**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Connect a flow">
        Until a published flow is connected, inbound calls are answered busy. See [Inbound and outbound routing](/telephony/inbound-and-outbound-routing).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API">
    API keys can look up countries, prices and available numbers. Search through the platform's **system provider** — list providers and take the one with `isSystemProvider: true`.

    Buying is done by the Owner in the dashboard: the purchase endpoint refuses API keys with `403 api_key_not_allowed`. Once the number is in your account, connect it to a flow with [`PATCH …/connect-flow`](/api-reference/phone-numbers/connect-flow).

## What uses a number

Open a number to see **Used by**: the flow its inbound calls go to, Meta lead form mappings that use it as caller ID, and campaigns and schedules that dial from it. *"Nothing uses this number yet."* when it's free. The API has the same list at `GET /phone/numbers/{id}/usage`.

## Releasing a number

Releasing gives the number back to the carrier and stops rental. It's permanent — a released number can't be recovered by you, and may later be issued to someone else. Only the Owner can release. From the number's page, **Release Number** opens *Release phone number*, which lists what releasing also stops; confirm with **Release number**, or keep it with **Keep number**. By API: [`DELETE /phone/numbers/{id}`](/api-reference/phone-numbers/release-phone-number).

- A number that's the **caller ID for a Meta Lead Ads form mapping** can't be released while the mapping exists — releasing it would silently stop calling new leads. The dashboard says *"This number can't be released yet"* and lists the forms; the API answers `409 cannot_delete_has_dependencies` with the same list. Pick another number on each form, or remove the mapping, then release.
- Releasing a Talkif number twice is harmless: the second request succeeds without effect.
- **A number in your own Twilio account stays in your Twilio account** when you remove it from Talkif. Talkif only clears the call settings that still point at Talkif; settings you made yourself are left alone.

There's no refund for the remainder of a rental period.
