A flow needs a line to live on. Talkif gives you three ways to get one, and they can be mixed freely in one account — a purchased US number for the support line, your existing SIP trunk for the numbers customers already know, and a WhatsApp Business number for the customers who'd rather not dial.

| Way | What you get | Outbound | Talkif telephony charge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Buy a number** | A new local number in a supported country, provisioned in seconds | yes | per minute + monthly rental |
| **Bring your own SIP trunk** | Your carrier's numbers, routed through Talkif over SIP | yes | per minute (lower rate); your carrier bills the PSTN leg |
| **Your own Twilio account** | Numbers you already hold in Twilio, used as-is | yes | per minute; Twilio bills you for the number |
| **WhatsApp Business Calling** | Inbound calls to your WhatsApp Business number | no — receive only | none; Meta bills the calling |

Whatever the source, a number in Talkif has the same lifecycle: it's **connected** to one published flow for inbound calls, it can be used as the **from** number for outbound calls if it has the voice capability, and it carries its own recording override.
