title: Regions and edges
description: Where Talkif runs, how a call is served from the region nearest the caller, how the carrier edge is chosen for a SIP trunk, and what to allowlist for signalling and media.
---

Latency is the enemy of a natural phone conversation, and a good part of it is geography: audio has to travel from the caller to the machine running the agent and back on every turn. Talkif runs the agent in two regions and serves each call from the one closer to the call, so that round trip stays short. You don't configure this — but if you bring your own carrier or run a firewall, you'll want to know how it works.

## Two regions

| Region | Location | Carrier edge |
|---|---|---|
| **EU** | Frankfurt (`eu-central-1`) | Frankfurt |
| **US** | Virginia (`us-east-1`) | Ashburn |

Both regions run the full platform: the API, the agents, and the media path. `api.talkif.ai` and the media endpoints are DNS-routed to the region with the lowest latency from where the request comes from. Your account, flows, numbers and call history are the same in both — there's one account, served from two places.

## Which region serves a call

- **Purchased numbers** are served through the carrier edge recorded on the number (`twilioEdge` / `twilioRegion` on the record), chosen when the number is bought.
- **SIP trunks**: when you create a SIP provider, Talkif looks at your carrier's IP addresses and picks the nearer region; if their location can't be determined, the US. Outbound calls on that provider's numbers are placed from that region. Inbound calls are handled in the region of the SIP address your carrier sends them to — see [Bring your own SIP trunk](/telephony/bring-your-own-sip-trunk#which-address-your-carrier-sees).
- **Your own Twilio account** numbers follow Twilio's edge for that number.
- **WhatsApp** calls arrive from Meta and are served from the nearer region.
- **Browser (WebRTC) test calls** connect to the nearer region from the browser's location.

An agent always runs in the same region as the call's media — there's no cross-region hop inside a call.

## What to allowlist

If your carrier or network filters traffic, these are the addresses involved. They differ for **signalling** (SIP) and **media** (RTP), which is the most common thing to get wrong.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="SIP signalling — your carrier ↔ Talkif" defaultOpen>
    Talkif has one SIP address per region, port **5060** (UDP, TCP or TLS as configured on your provider): **`sip.talkif.ai`** = **`63.176.211.13`** in the EU and **`sip.us.talkif.ai`** = **`44.218.212.38`** in the US. Outbound INVITEs come *from* the address of your provider's region; inbound calls can be sent *to* either. Allowlist both on your carrier's trunk if you're not sure which region your provider is in. Talkif accepts inbound SIP only from the carrier IPs listed on your provider.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Media (RTP) — your carrier ↔ Talkif">
    Media doesn't flow through the signalling address. On a SIP trunk, audio is exchanged with Talkif's media servers in the region that handles the call, on UDP ports **40000–49999**; each call's SDP gives the address to send it to. These addresses aren't fixed, and a region can use more than one, so don't limit RTP to a fixed list of Talkif addresses — let your carrier send media to the address in the SDP. Symptom of getting this wrong: calls connect, nobody hears anything (or only one side does).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Webhooks and flow functions — Talkif → your servers">
    HTTPS requests from Talkif to your endpoints (flow functions, webhooks) leave from a fixed set of egress addresses per region. See [Network and IP allowlists](/reference/network-and-ip-allowlists) for the current list — and verify the `Talkif-Signature` header rather than relying on source IP alone.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Data residency

Your data — recordings, transcripts, contacts, insights — is stored once, in Talkif's EU primary datastore, with a replica in the US region for latency; recordings are held in EU object storage. Serving a call from the US region does not create a separate US copy of your account data. For questions about residency commitments, contact support.
