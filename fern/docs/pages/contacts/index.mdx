Contacts are the people your AI voice agents call. Talkif provides a centralized contact database with tagging, custom fields, bulk import, and DNC compliance.

## Capabilities

- **Create and manage contacts** — store name, phone numbers, email, company, occupation, tags, and interests
- **Tags** — categorize contacts with free-form labels
- **Bulk import** — upload CSV, JSON, or vCard files with phone normalization and duplicate detection
- **Do Not Call** — maintain a phone-number-based DNC list that blocks outbound calls automatically
