title: Phone Numbers
description: Purchase, configure, and manage phone numbers for your voice interactions.
---

Phone numbers are the entry point for all voice interactions in Talkif. Every inbound call arrives through a phone number, and every outbound call is placed from one.

## Key concepts

- **Purchase numbers** directly from Talkif or bring your own carrier
- **Assign flows** — each number can be linked to a published flow that handles its calls
- **BYOC** — connect your existing Twilio numbers or SIP infrastructure
