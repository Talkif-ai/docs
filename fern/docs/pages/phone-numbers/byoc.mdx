title: Bring Your Own Carrier
description: Connect your existing Twilio numbers or SIP infrastructure to Talkif.
---

Use your existing telephony infrastructure with Talkif's flow engine. Three integration paths are available: Twilio BYOC, SIP, and WhatsApp Business Calling.

## Twilio BYOC

Connect your Twilio account to use existing Twilio numbers with Talkif.

### Setup

1. **Create a provider** — add a new provider (type: OwnTwilio) with your Twilio **Account SID** and **Auth Token**, both found on the [Twilio Console](https://console.twilio.com) dashboard
2. **List available numbers** — Talkif reads the phone numbers on your Twilio account so you can browse them
3. **Register selected numbers** — choose which numbers to bring into Talkif

Registering does not move numbers out of your Twilio account — they stay yours, and unregistering only detaches them from Talkif. Once registered, your Twilio numbers appear in the platform and can be assigned to flows like any other number.

## SIP Integration

Route SIP traffic through Talkif's flow engine using your existing SIP trunk. Setup is two-way: you give Talkif your carrier's SIP details, and Talkif gives you back a **termination URI** that you point your trunk at.

### Step 1 — Create a SIP provider

Add a new provider (type: **SIP**) with your carrier's details:

| Field | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `sipDomain` | Yes | Your carrier's SIP host, e.g. `sip.mycarrier.com` |
| `sipUsername` | Yes | Trunk auth username |
| `sipPassword` | Yes | Trunk auth password |
| `sipProtocol` | Yes | `UDP`, `TCP`, or `TLS` |
| `providerIps` | No | Your carrier's source IPs. If omitted, Talkif resolves them from `sipDomain` via DNS |

### Step 2 — Point your trunk at Talkif

When the provider is created, Talkif provisions a dedicated ingress and returns a **termination URI** of the form:

```
talkif-<id>.sip.<edge>.twilio.com
```

Configure this URI as the **origination/termination target** on your carrier's trunk. This is the address your carrier sends inbound calls to so they reach Talkif. Talkif authenticates the leg using credentials it generates for you automatically — you don't create those.

The `<edge>` is chosen automatically from your carrier's location (e.g. `ashburn`, `frankfurt`) so media takes the shortest path.

### Step 3 — Register phone numbers

Add your phone numbers (E.164, e.g. `+15551234567`) against the SIP provider. SIP numbers activate **immediately** — no verification call is required. Once registered, assign a published flow and inbound SIP traffic is handled by that flow.

## WhatsApp Business Calling

Connect your WhatsApp Business Account and let agents answer inbound WhatsApp calls. Connect via Meta's Embedded Signup or by pasting WABA credentials, then register numbers under the account. WhatsApp numbers are receive-only, and Meta bills the calling directly — there is no telephony charge in Talkif.

See [WhatsApp](/phone-numbers/whatsapp) for setup, number status lifecycle, and pricing.
